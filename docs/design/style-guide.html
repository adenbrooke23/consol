Design System · v2.0 · July 2026

CONSOLE.

The home playback hub. Play anything, anywhere: the game on the den TV, a movie in the lounge, jazz in the study — each one a session card you create, control, and dismiss. Not a streaming app, not a dashboard: an instrument for routing media through a house.

01

The model: sessions, not rooms

Earlier drafts treated rooms as fixed channel strips. That's a speaker mental model, and this product is bigger than speakers. The unit of this app is a session: something playing somewhere — a WHAT (game, movie, album, playlist) bound to a WHERE (a TV, a speaker, or a group of speakers).

A card is a session. Cards are born (tap +, pick what, pick where), they live on the home stage while playing, and they die (swipe down) — releasing their devices back to the pool. Devices are resources, not destinations you visit: each device belongs to at most one card at a time, and the home screen is simply the list of everything currently happening in the house. Nothing playing = empty stage.

The three standing rules

Signal: Bright Lemon means signal present — meters, the ● beside a live card's location, focus, live readouts under the finger, the online dot. Never a fill on a control face; ≤3% of any screen. Touch: white marks what you grab — the play squircle, a bar while touched. Nothing else is solid white. Stage: the canvas is true black. Cards are hardware modules sitting on a dark stage; album and poster art are the only saturated color besides lemon. No shadows, gloss, blur, or gradients in chrome — depth is tonal steps and hairlines.

04

Home: the stage & the empty state

Signed in with nothing playing, the answer to "what does the app look like" is: almost nothing. A black stage, one dashed new-session card breathing gently in the center, and a single mono instruction low on the canvas. The emptiness is the design — a switched-off console invites you to switch something on.

Tap to play something, anywhere in the house

With sessions running, the stage is a row of cards — every card is something happening in the house right now. Tap a card to focus it. The dashed + card always sits at the end of the row, smaller, ready. A 36pt status strip anchors the bottom:

05

Creating a card — try it

Tap the + and the dashed card becomes a chooser, in place: step 1, WHAT (Music · Live TV & Sports · Movies & Shows · Apps) — step 2, WHERE (every device in the house with its live status). Choose both and the chooser becomes the card, scaling in as the session starts. Two taps, no navigation, and it reads the way you'd say it: put the game on the den TV.

06

Session cards

Anatomy, top to bottom: WHERE (mono label, lemon ● when live) + KIND tag · art (16:10 for video, 1:1 for music — shape encodes kind) · title / subline · a footer that matches the kind. Focused cards reveal full controls; resting cards stay glanceable.

Skip glyphs compress to 80% and spring back; the play/pause glyph morphs inside the fixed squircle. Bars have no thumb — the bar is the handle: it inflates under the finger, the fill turns white, the readout turns lemon, then exhales on release. Gestures first: swipe art sideways to skip track / change channel; drag art vertically for volume. Keys are the fallback, not the primary interface.

Search lives inside the card, so the "play where?" question never exists — you're searching where you're standing. Artist art is circular, albums square. Tapping the artist line in now-playing flips the card straight into that artist's page. Long-press a result for Play next · Queue · Play on… (the only zone picker in the app).

Do / Don't

DO
  • One focused card at a time; siblings stay live
  • Lemon as indication only: meters, live dots, focus, readouts in motion
  • White for what you grab: the play squircle, bars while touched
  • Mono + lemon for every live value (dB, timecode, clock)
  • Shape encodes kind: 16:10 video art, square music art, circular artists
  • Bare glyphs for transport; gestures first, keys as fallback
DON'T
  • No shadows, glassmorphism, blur, or gradients in chrome
  • No lemon fills on control faces; no lemon text on white
  • No radius above 16pt; no pill-shaped cards
  • No navigation away from the stage for playback tasks
  • No keypad grids, no unicode/emoji transport glyphs
  • No Inter/Roboto/system-default type as brand faces
07

Navigation & sign-in

7.1 · Edge handle → vertical tabs

Content · full bleed Collapsed: nothing but a 4pt edge handle. Tap it — tabs bloom from the bezel. Tap the active tab to collapse.

Four fixed tabs — Stage · Search · Devices · Profile — vertical, transparent (no container: icons sit on the black canvas), collapsing to a 4×36pt edge handle: the home-indicator idiom rotated vertical. Grouping left the tab set — it's a property of music cards (member chips), not a destination. Devices is the registry: every output, its status, its owner card, hub diagnostics. Per HIG the tab set never changes with context; on iPhone the same four render as a standard opaque bottom tab bar via adaptive TabView.

7.2 · Sign-in — try it

The login screen is the app at its most reduced: a black screen, the wordmark, one line of instruction, four boxes. No profile picker — the PIN is the identity. Each family member has their own PIN (uniqueness enforced at setup), so entering it lands you directly in your profile: your recents, your queue rights, done. Digits come from the system numeric keyboard (or a hardware keypad on a wall mount); boxes fill left to right with a scaling dot, the active box carries a lavender border. Wrong PIN: the row shakes once, borders flash alert, boxes empty. Correct: borders flash lemon, then the wordmark cross-fades into the stage.

7.3 · Devices tab

The house's output registry, one row per device: name (Label style), kind, and mono status — FREE with a lemon dot, or the owning card's title. Tapping a busy device jumps to its card; tapping a free one starts the chooser at WHAT with WHERE pre-filled — creation works from either end of the sentence.

Motion

One hero move per surface, mechanical micro-feedback, ambient meters only where audio is live. Springs over curves; bounce stays ≤0.15 — playful reads as toy, not instrument.

Handoff checklist

① Lift the token block 1:1 into a SwiftUI Theme. ② License Söhne + Berkeley Mono or ship General Sans + Martian Mono. ③ Build the Session store + one card first; the chooser is the same component ending in a different state. ④ The §08 motion table is the contract — nothing animates outside it in v1.
